Cart Corral — Terms & Privacy Notice

Last updated: this is a plain-language document written for a small community project, not reviewed by a lawyer. If Cart Corral ever grows beyond a personal/hobby project, replace this with a proper legal review.

Cart Corral is an independent, unofficial tool. It is not made by, run by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Target Corporation in any way. It exists to help community members report Target-owned shopping carts left in parking lots, streets, or other public areas so they can be recovered.

What this app does

Cart Corral lets logged-in users drop a map pin at their current location to report a stray shopping cart. Other users can report near that same pin to confirm it's really there. Confirmed reports earn both people points on a public leaderboard.

What data we collect

  • Account info: the email, password, and patrol name (username) you sign up with.
  • Location data: the GPS coordinates of your device at the moment you tap "report" or confirm a pin. We do not track your location in the background or at any other time — only when you actively submit a report.
  • Optional notes: any text you add to a report.

How your data is used and shared

Reported cart locations (coordinates, timestamps, and confirmation status) may be shared with retailers — including Target — to help them locate and recover their shopping carts. This is a core purpose of the app: your reports are meant to get carts back to stores. Location data is not sold to third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Your username and point total are visible to other logged-in users on the leaderboard. Your email and password are never shown to other users.

Accuracy and honor system

Cart Corral relies on an honor system for accuracy — we don't verify with a photo that a reported cart actually belongs to Target, or that it's still there by the time anyone reads a report. Please report in good faith. Any user can flag a report they believe is fake, wrong, or spam; reports that receive multiple flags are automatically hidden from the map pending review.

Account & data deletion

You can request your account and associated data be deleted by contacting the app's administrator. Reports you've submitted may remain in aggregate/anonymized form if needed to keep historical cart-recovery data useful to stores.

No warranty

Cart Corral is provided as-is, with no guarantee of accuracy, uptime, or that any reported cart will actually be recovered. Don't rely on it for anything safety-critical.

Changes

These terms may change as the app develops. Continued use after a change means you accept the update.
